Preparing a return for two unopened items from the Blake bathroom remodel.

Assisted Return Process
  1. Identify unused or returnable materials from the project
  2. Match each item to its original purchase
  3. Locate the digital receipt and PO reference
  4. Confirm item condition (unopened, unused)
  5. Check illustrative return eligibility
  6. Identify likely return method (in-store, locker, mail)
  7. Estimate an illustrative credit value
  8. Prepare a return summary for contractor review
  9. Contractor confirms items and quantities
  10. Return summary handed off to Home Depot's return process
  11. Project reconciliation updates once the return completes

Summary

Final Value Summary

Home Depot helps the Pro price and fulfill the materials. QuickEstimate helps the Pro price and manage the entire job.

For the Pro
  • One connected view of estimate, materials, purchasing, and job status
  • Less time spent shopping, reconciling receipts, and tracking down orders
  • Fewer pricing mistakes and forgotten costs eating into margin
  • Faster reordering and change orders without starting over
  • Clear visibility into what's owned, used, returned, or available
For the Customer
  • A clear, itemized proposal with real product detail, not vague line items
  • Confidence that pricing and materials are tied to an actual purchase plan
  • Faster answers when a delay or substitution comes up
  • Simple electronic approval for both the original scope and any changes
For Home Depot
  • Deeper Pro Xtra engagement across the life of a project, not just a single order
  • More attributed purchases, repeat purchases, and reorders
  • A stronger connection to the contractor's day-to-day workflow
  • A differentiated Pro experience built around the whole job, not just the cart
QuickEstimate can connect the contractor's customer experience with Home Depot's Pro purchasing and fulfillment ecosystem.
